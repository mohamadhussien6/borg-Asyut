# Borg Asyut — برج أسيوط

**Developer / Creative Director:** محمد الحسين (Mohamad El Hussein)

**Genre:** 2D arcade / endless platformer / single-player

**Language:** Egyptian Arabic (interface and voice); English store description.

## English short description
Leap across the rooftops of Asyut, bounce off walls, chain combos and beat your personal best in an Egyptian arcade platformer.

## English full description
The chase is on! A man from Upper Egypt gets caught up in an artifact-smuggling scheme, and his only way out is up. Climb old balconies, mosaic rooftops and red-brick buildings in a fast-paced arcade game inspired by the streets of Asyut.

Build momentum, bounce off walls, skip floors and chain combos. Survive the rising camera across four connected height zones, with Egyptian Arabic menus, Saidi voices and a locally saved personal high score.

Created by Mohamad El Hussein — game development, game design, creative direction and production.

## Controls
A/D or arrows: move; Space: jump; Escape: pause/resume; R: restart after game over. Windows: Alt+Enter toggles display mode.
